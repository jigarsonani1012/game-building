How to Play
Official Rules & Guide
Starting & Moving
Roll a 6 to move a pawn out of base. Move clockwise. Rolling a 6 gives an extra turn. (Three 6s in a row ends your turn).
Capturing Opponents
Land on an opponent's pawn to capture it, sending it back to base. Capturing awards you a Bonus Roll.
Safe Zones
Pawns on squares marked with a Star (★) are in a safe zone and cannot be captured by opponents.
Winning the Game
Be the first to move all 4 pawns into the center Home triangle. You must roll the exact number to enter Home.
3D Controls
Drag to orbit, Pinch/Scroll to zoom, and Double-Tap to reset the camera view.
