How to play
  • • Tap one of your pieces, then tap a highlighted square.
  • • Men move one step diagonally forward.
  • • Jump over an adjacent enemy piece to capture it.
  • • Capturing is mandatory, and multi-jumps must be finished.
  • • Reach the far row to be crowned a king — kings move and jump in every direction.
  • • Win by capturing every enemy piece or blocking all of their moves.
  • • Switch between the 3D table and the classic 2D board at any time.
