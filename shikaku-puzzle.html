Rules in 30 seconds

Draw rectangles.
Respect the numbers.

4
1 · The number is the area.

A clue “4” must live inside a rectangle of exactly 4 cells — 1×4, 4×1 or 2×2.

6
2 · Exactly one clue per rectangle.

This 3×2 rectangle holds one “6”. Two clues inside? Invalid. Zero? Invalid.

3
3
3 · No gaps, no overlaps.

Every cell belongs to exactly one rectangle. The whole grid becomes a clean parcel map.

Edge logic — your first deduction

A “5” sitting two cells from the right wall can't stretch 5-wide to the right — the wall forbids it. Corners are even stricter. Experts read the walls before they draw.

Controls. Drag across cells, or tap two opposite corners. Tap a placed rectangle to select it, then Delete or Erase. Ctrl Z undo · H hint · R reset · Esc cancel.
