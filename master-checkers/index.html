OFFICIAL RULES
HOW TO PLAY CHECKERS
DIAGONAL STEPS

Standard Movement

Checkers is played exclusively on the 32 dark squares of the board. Normal pieces advance 1 step diagonally forward into an unoccupied adjacent square.

SMART CONTROLS

Selecting & Moving

Tap any piece to select it. Glowing green target markers highlight all legal destinations. Tap the green marker to move smoothly.

MANDATORY RULE

Jumping & Capturing

When an opponent piece is diagonally adjacent and the square directly behind it is empty, you must jump over and capture it.

⚠️
Strict Rule: If any capture is available on your turn, you must take it. Standard non-jump moves are prohibited until all captures are resolved.
MULTI-JUMPS

Continuous Chain Jumps

If a piece lands from a jump and another jump is immediately open, you must continue jumping in the exact same turn until all consecutive captures are completed.

PROMOTION

Crowning a King 👑

When a regular piece reaches the opponent's farthest back row (the King's Row), it is promoted and crowned with the royal emblem.

360° POWER

Backwards & Forwards Diagonal

Unlike normal pieces that only step forward, Kings can move and jump both forwards and backwards diagonally, making them formidable attackers.

WINNING

How to Win

  • Total Wipeout: Capture all 12 of your opponent's pieces.
  • Trap & Blockade: Surround and block your opponent so they have zero legal moves remaining.
